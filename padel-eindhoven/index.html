Never cancel a padel court again

Court booked, then a friend drops out? Instead of cancelling, post the open spot. Players at your level request to join and you choose who plays.

Who plays on OneShort?

Beginners who just picked up a racket, regulars looking for a steady fourth, and students and internationals in Eindhoven who don't have a padel group yet.

What does it cost?

OneShort is free to use. The host lists the cost per player for the court, so you know your share before you request a spot.

Is my phone number safe?

Your number is only shared with the host of the game you request, and you see theirs once they approve you.
